How We Grow

Our Growing Process

1

Selecting Healthy Mother Stock

Only strong, disease-free parent plants are chosen for propagation — the foundation of everything we grow.

2

Cutting & Propagation

Cuttings are taken and rooted under careful humidity and temperature control for high success rates.

3

Root Development

Young plants spend weeks in nursery beds building the strong root systems that make them resilient.

4

Potting in Breathable Soil

Each plant is potted in a loose, well-draining organic mix that keeps roots healthy long-term.

5

Hardening Off

Plants are gradually exposed to real-world conditions before leaving the greenhouse.

6

Final Quality Check

Every plant is individually inspected for pests, leaf health, and root strength before dispatch.

"We don't rush the roots."
